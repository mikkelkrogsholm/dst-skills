## Executive Summary

Denmark's electric vehicle adoption has accelerated dramatically, with EVs reaching **21.15%** of the total vehicle fleet by September 2025, up from virtually zero (0.004%) in 1993. Pure battery electric vehicles (BEVs) now represent **16.74%** of the fleet, with the remaining 4.42% being hybrids.

### Key Milestones
- **1993:** 65 EVs (0.004% of fleet)
- **2015:** 2,919 EVs (0.13% of fleet) - Early adoption phase
- **2020:** 25,345 EVs (0.96% of fleet) - 1% threshold crossed
- **2022:** 144,468 EVs (5.18% of fleet) - Rapid acceleration begins
- **2024:** 323,108 EVs (11.43% of fleet) - 10% threshold crossed
- **Sep 2025:** 615,764 EVs (21.15% of fleet) - Over 1 in 5 vehicles

## BEV vs Hybrid Split

### Fleet Composition (December 2024)
- **Pure BEV:** 344,440 vehicles (73% of EVs)
- **Hybrids:** 127,566 vehicles (27% of EVs)
- **Total EVs:** 472,006 vehicles

### September 2025
- **Pure BEV:** 487,140 vehicles (79% of EVs)
- **Hybrids:** 128,624 vehicles (21% of EVs)
- **Total EVs:** 615,764 vehicles

**Key Insight:** Pure battery electric vehicles are increasingly dominant, growing from 73% to 79% of the EV fleet in just 9 months. Hybrid count is relatively stagnant while BEV adoption accelerates.

## Data Quality Assessment

### Strengths
- **Comprehensive coverage:** 33 years of annual data, 14+ years of monthly data
- **Multiple dimensions:** Ownership, fuel type, weight class
- **Recent updates:** Monthly data updated October 2025
- **Validation:** No suppressed values found in key EV categories

### Limitations
- **Category mismatch:** BIL10 uses "Pluginhybrid" while BIL52/BIL51 use "Benzinhybrid" + "Dieselhybrid"
- **2025 data:** Annual BIL10 data for 2025 appears to be full-year estimate
- **Suppressed values:** Some minor categories have ".." (suppressed) values

### Data Handling
- All suppressed values (".." strings) were excluded from calculations
- Aggregate codes ("Drivmidler i alt", "I alt") were filtered appropriately
- Numeric casting was performed safely with CASE statements
- Time-based sorting handled string formats correctly (2024M01, 2024M02, etc.)

### Recommended Regression Models

**1. Logistic Growth Model (S-curve)**
```
EV_share = L / (1 + exp(-k(t - t0)))
```
- Best for modeling technology adoption
- Accounts for market saturation
- Use annual data (1993-2025) for long-term trend

**2. Time Series Regression (ARIMA/SARIMA)**
- Use monthly data for short-term forecasting
- Account for seasonality in new registrations
- Model acceleration/deceleration phases

**3. Segmented Regression**
Identify breakpoints:
- 2015-2019: Slow growth phase

- Future: Approaching saturation?

## Interesting Trends and Anomalies

### 1. The "Hybrid Hump" (2020-2023)
Plugin hybrids surged 2020-2022 but have plateaued since 2023. New hybrid registrations collapsed from 40K (2021) to 4K (2025). This suggests:
- Hybrids were a transitional technology
- Buyers are now going directly to pure BEVs
- Charging infrastructure and range anxiety have diminished

### 2. Total Fleet Size Stability
Despite EV growth, total fleet size is remarkably stable:
- 2020: 2,651,725 vehicles
- 2025: 2,910,735 vehicles
- Growth: Only 9.8% in 5 years

This indicates EV adoption is primarily replacing ICE vehicles, not adding to fleet size.

### 3. Seasonal Patterns in New Registrations
Monthly data shows strong December peaks in new registrations, likely due to:
- Year-end tax incentives
- Dealer clearance sales
- Business fleet renewal cycles

### 4. Acceleration of Acceleration (2024-2025)
Monthly growth rates are increasing:
- 2024: +0.4-0.5 percentage points per month
- 2025: +0.5-0.6 percentage points per month

This suggests Denmark may be entering a "tipping point" phase where EV adoption becomes self-reinforcing through network effects.

## Technical Notes

### SQL Patterns Used
- **Suppressed value handling:** `CASE WHEN INDHOLD != '..' THEN CAST(INDHOLD AS INTEGER)`
- **Aggregate filtering:** `WHERE DRIV NOT IN ('Drivmidler i alt', ...)`
- **Time parsing:** `SUBSTRING(TID, 1, 4)` for year extraction
- **Window functions:** `LAG() OVER (ORDER BY year)` for growth calculations
- **CTEs:** Extensive use of WITH clauses for readability

### Data Validation Checks Performed
- Verified no suppressed values in key EV categories
- Cross-referenced BIL10 annual totals with BIL52 monthly year-end values
- Confirmed ownership totals match "I alt" (total) values
- Validated that new registrations accumulate to fleet changes

1. **BIL10 2025 data:** Appears to be full-year estimate rather than partial year

## Appendix: Data Dictionary

### BIL10 Variables
- **DRIV:** Fuel type (Drivmidler)
  - El: Pure electric (BEV)
  - Pluginhybrid: Plugin hybrid (PHEV)
  - Benzin: Gasoline
  - Diesel: Diesel
  - Others: Brint, F-gas, N-gas, Petroleum, Øvrige drivmidler
  - Drivmidler i alt: Total (aggregate)
- **EGEN:** Weight class (Egenvægt)
  - I alt: Total (aggregate)
  - 0-600 kg, 601-700 kg, ..., Over 2.000 kg, Uoplyst (unknown)
- **TID:** Year (1993-2025)
- **INDHOLD:** Count of vehicles

### BIL52/BIL51 Variables
- **EJER:** Owner type
  - I alt: Total (aggregate)
  - Husholdningerne: Households
  - Erhvervene: Business/Commercial
- **DRIV:** Fuel type
  - El: Pure electric (BEV)
  - Benzinhybrid: Gasoline hybrid
  - Dieselhybrid: Diesel hybrid
  - Others: Benzin, Diesel, Brint, F-gas, N-gas, Petroleum, Ætanol, Metanol
  - Drivmidler i alt: Total (aggregate)
- **TID:** Month code (YYYYMNN format, e.g., 2024M01)
- **INDHOLD:** Count (stock in BIL52, new registrations in BIL51)

### Calculated Variables
- **bev_count:** Pure battery electric vehicles (DRIV = 'El')
- **phev_count:** Plugin hybrid electric vehicles (DRIV = 'Pluginhybrid' in BIL10)
- **hybrid_count:** All hybrid types (DRIV IN ('Benzinhybrid', 'Dieselhybrid') in BIL52/BIL51)
- **ev_total:** Total electric vehicles (BEV + hybrids)
- **total_vehicles:** Total fleet size (all fuel types except aggregates)
- **ev_share_pct:** EV percentage of total fleet
- **bev_share_pct:** BEV percentage of total fleet
- **ev_growth:** Absolute year-over-year EV increase
- **ev_growth_pct:** Percentage year-over-year EV growth rate
