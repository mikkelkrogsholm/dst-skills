Key Insights

  • Exponential Growth: EV adoption accelerated dramatically after 2019, growing from 0.59% to 16.48% in just 6 years
  • Recent Surge: Between 2024 and 2025 alone, EV share increased by 5.05 percentage points - the largest single-year growth on record
  • Battery Electric Leading: Pure battery electric vehicles (344,439) now outnumber plug-in hybrids (127,565) by nearly 3:1
  • Market Maturity: With nearly 1 in 6 cars now electric, Denmark is approaching mainstream EV adoption
  • Historical Perspective: It took 27 years (1993-2020) to reach 1% EV share, but only 5 more years to reach 16%
